## Usage rules

- Photographs appear **only** on the public landing and unauthenticated doorway screens (`/`, `/login`, `/join`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`).
- Role workspaces (`/director`, `/priest`, `/admin`) use flat Warm Paper (`#F5F0E8`), Sheet White (`#FFFFFF`), and Ivory (`#F7F4ED`) surfaces with zero background photography.
